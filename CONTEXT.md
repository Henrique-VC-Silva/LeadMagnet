# Context

Glossary of terms used in the Lead Magnet Roulette project.

## Glossary

### Lead
A potential customer's contact information (Mandatory Email, optional Name and Phone Number) captured by the system in exchange for a spin of the Roulette. Under GDPR, a Lead must provide explicit Consent.

### Consent
The explicit, freely given, and informed agreement provided by a Lead (typically via a checkbox) allowing the system to store their data and send them the Prize and marketing materials.

### Roulette
The interactive game component where a Lead spins a virtual wheel to determine which Prize they have won. Leads are permitted to re-engage (spin again) with the same Email address.

### Prize
A reward (e.g., a discount code, a free resource, or a physical item) that is assigned to a segment of the Roulette and awarded to a Lead.

### No Prize
A specific segment of the Roulette that results in no reward. If a Lead lands on a No Prize segment, they are permitted to spin again immediately using the same contact information.

### Probability Weight
A numerical value assigned to a Prize that determines its likelihood of being selected relative to other available Prizes.

### Stock
The finite number of times a specific Prize can be awarded. Once the Stock is depleted, the Prize is no longer eligible to be won.

### Lead Magnet
The application as a whole, serving the purpose of attracting and capturing Leads through an interactive experience.

### Retention Policy
A configurable rule that determines the maximum duration a Lead's data is stored. Once the data exceeds this duration (e.g., 90 days), it is automatically purged to ensure GDPR compliance.

### Admin
A user account with privileged access to the management dashboard, allowed to configure Prizes, view/export Leads, and set the Retention Policy.

### Copy
Dynamic text elements (e.g., title, subtitle, button text) stored in the database, allowing Admins to rewrite the interface for different campaigns without code changes.

### Campaign
A core structural marketing entity that isolates styling themes (custom color palettes and background images), associated Prizes, and captured Leads for a specific marketing effort or version of the Roulette.
